01 / About

About

I am an economics researcher based in Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, currently working as a Research Assistant at the Centre for Development Studies (CDS) under the guidance of Professor Vinoj Abraham.

My research interests lie at the intersection of labour economics and development economics. I am particularly interested in understanding how socioeconomic characteristics and structural inequalities shape labour market opportunities and outcomes, especially in developing economies.

My current research examines unemployment duration and intensity in India, with a focus on capturing dimensions of employment distress that are often overlooked by conventional unemployment measures.

I hold an M.A. in Economics from the University of Hyderabad (2024) and a B.A. in Economics from University College, University of Kerala (2021).

02 / Research areas

What I'm working on.

My work combines quantitative analysis, survey-based research and policy-oriented questions, with a focus on the Indian economy.

01

Unemployment intensity in India

Studying unemployment duration and severity using PLFS microdata and developing an index that captures distress beyond the unemployment rate.

02

Urban heat and productivity

Analysing primary and secondary data using Stata, including regression-based estimation and contributions to a heat vulnerability index.

03

Plantation development

Research on the political economy and long-term yield trends of tea, coffee, rubber and spices, including policy briefs and stakeholder engagement.

03 / Writing

Research & publications.

Working paper · October 2026

Measuring Unemployment Intensity: A Study on the Indian Labour Force

Vinoj Abraham and Anagha Soman

Examines unemployment spells and long-term unemployment in India, develops an intensity index combining incidence and duration, and studies differences across socioeconomic groups.

Journal article · 2025

“The World Is Not Ready to Accept Us”

Anagha Soman, Maheswari, K. Ragasuriya and G. Sridevi · Empirical Economics Letters, 24 (May Special Issue 4), 29–39

Based on primary survey research with 50 transgender participants, exploring socioeconomic challenges and employment experiences.

Master's thesis · May 2024

Education and Employment Gap in Kerala: An Analysis of the Higher Education Sector

University of Hyderabad · Advisor: Prof. K. Laxminarayana

Examines education, employment trends and skill mismatches using quantitative and qualitative approaches and NSSO/PLFS data.
